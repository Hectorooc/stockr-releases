STOCKR DISCLAIMER

Stockr consolidates figures that companies report to the U.S. SEC in XBRL and computes ratios, scores (Altman, Piotroski, Beneish, synthetic ratings), valuations and, if you choose, AI-generated summaries.

- Data can be wrong or incomplete: companies tag figures differently, restate them, or leave gaps; Stockr's consolidation rules and data checks reduce but don't remove these problems. Always check important figures against the original filing (Stockr links to every one).
- Share prices come from the provider you choose; their accuracy is that provider's responsibility.
- Credit and fraud-risk models are statistical screens, not ratings or findings. A low score is not proof of distress or manipulation, and a high score is not a guarantee.
- A DCF is only as good as its assumptions.
- AI summaries can misread or omit information. Read the filing before relying on them.
